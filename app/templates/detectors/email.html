What gets analysed

Sender
Look-alike and free-mail domains, display-name spoofing, brand in subdomain, Reply-To / Return-Path mismatch
Subject
Urgency, threats, suspension, verification demands, reward bait, shouting, fake reply prefixes
Body
Credential and OTP requests, financial instructions, gift-card fraud, secrecy pressure, authority claims, attachment lures, QR prompts
Links
Full URL analysis of every embedded link, plus anchor-text vs href mismatch and shorteners
Headers
SPF, DKIM, DMARC verdicts, Received-chain length, bulk-mailer fingerprints

Privacy handling

  • Submitted bodies are redacted (passwords, OTPs, tokens, card numbers) and truncated before storage.
  • The audit log records the verdict and score only — never the message content.
  • Nothing is sent to a third-party service; all analysis is local.
