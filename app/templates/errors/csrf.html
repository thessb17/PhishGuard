CSRF validation failed

PhishGuard rejected this request because it did not carry a valid anti-forgery token. Nothing was changed. This protection is what stops another website from submitting forms on your behalf.

Why this usually happens

  • The page was open longer than the token lifetime and the token expired.
  • Your browser session expired or was cleared.
  • Your browser is blocking cookies for this site.
  • The page was restored from cache or the back button after signing out.
  • The application was restarted with a different secret key, invalidating old sessions.
  • The app is embedded in a frame on another site, and your browser is withholding the session cookie from that frame.

What to do

Reloading the page issues a fresh token, which resolves almost every case.
