Reporting guidance

  1. Use the report phishing button in your mail client — do not forward the message as plain text, because that strips the headers analysts need.
  2. If there is no button, attach the original message (.eml or .msg) to a new email addressed to your security team.
  3. Say whether you clicked, downloaded anything or entered information anywhere. Speed matters far more than embarrassment.
  4. Delete the message afterwards, and do not warn colleagues by forwarding the original.
Reporting is blame-free. Security teams would rather review ten false alarms than miss the one real intrusion.
